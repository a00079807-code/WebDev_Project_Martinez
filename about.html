• Snow Trails Travel Agency exists for one reason: to connect passionate riders and skiers with the world's best snow.

Our founder, Lucas Snow, has spent his life chasing winter. From carving down fresh corduroy to exploring hidden backcountry bowls, nothing stops him from pursuing the next alpine adventure. To share this passion and streamline the booking process for fellow riders, he established Snow Trails.
We exist to connect you with the world's finest snow-covered destinations. We handle premium resort bookings, ski passes, and custom itineraries so you can focus entirely on the thrill of the descent.
We don't just book trips. We help you create stories worth telling around the fire.
